    What this demonstrates

    One change updates the whole plan.

    In a finished Multilig Planner workflow, procedure selection, patient anatomy, tunnels, fixation and the planned operative sequence remain connected. The demonstration uses simplified sample geometry but illustrates the intended interaction model.
